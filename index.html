MindEase Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

MindEase ("the app") is designed so that your personal reflections and generated hypnosis sessions never leave your device.

What stays on your device

  • The problem category and any free-text details you enter to generate a session.
  • The generated hypnosis script itself.
  • Your language preference, narration speed, and whether you've purchased "Remove Ads".

All script generation runs entirely on-device using a locally-stored AI model. Your input and the generated script are never transmitted to MindEase's developer or any third party.

What does leave your device

  • Advertising: unless you've purchased "Remove Ads", the app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data per Google's own privacy policy to serve and measure ads. MindEase does not send your session content, category selection, or free-text input to AdMob or any ad network.
  • Purchases: the "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing; standard Google Play purchase data applies (see Google's privacy policy).
  • Model download: on first use, the app downloads the AI model weights (~1.3GB) over the network. This is a one-way download of app functionality, not a data upload — no user data is sent as part of this download.

Data retention and deletion

Since your inputs and generated scripts are stored only in the app's local storage, uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is no server-side account or profile to delete because none exists.

Children's privacy

MindEase is not directed at children and is not intended for users under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
