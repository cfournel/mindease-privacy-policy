Self-hypnosis for waking up in the night

A session for the 3 a.m. wake-up: the racing thought, the clock you cannot stop checking, the dread of the morning.

Why falling back asleep is the hard part

Waking briefly in the night is normal. What keeps you awake is what happens next: checking the time, counting the hours left, getting a little more alert with each calculation. The effort to sleep is itself what wakes you.

A session meant for the middle of the night is slow and low on purpose. It gives your attention somewhere gentle to rest, takes the pressure off sleeping, and does not ask you to do anything.

What a session works on

  • Letting go of the clock and the arithmetic of how much sleep is left.
  • Quieting the thought that started the wake-up.
  • Relaxing the body without asking it to try.
  • Drifting back without being brought up to alertness at the end.

How Onira builds your session

  1. You pick a theme and, if you want, type a sentence or two about what is actually going on.
  2. An AI model writes the script — induction, deepening, imagery, suggestions, anchoring, then a clear return to full alertness. It can run entirely on your phone, offline, or on Onira Cloud if you prefer.
  3. Narration starts as soon as the opening is ready and the rest keeps writing while you listen, so a full 15 to 30 minute session begins in seconds.
  4. Keep a session you liked and replay the exact same narration whenever you want.

What to expect

Keep the volume low and your phone face-down. Sessions are meant to be played to the end or until you fall asleep. Several weeks of the same routine usually matter more than any one night.

Common questions

Can I fall asleep during a session?

That is the point. A sleep session is written so that you can drift off, and nothing is lost if you do.

What if I wake up at the same hour every night?

A regular pattern is worth mentioning to a doctor, especially with loud snoring, pain, or daytime exhaustion. A session can ease the waking, not diagnose it.

Should I look at my phone at 3 a.m.?

Dim the screen, start the session, and put the phone face-down. Avoid anything else on it.
