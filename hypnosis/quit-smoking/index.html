Self-hypnosis to quit smoking

A session that works on the moment of the craving — the trigger, the ritual, the reason you are quitting — rather than on willpower.

Why hypnosis is used for quitting smoking

Smoking is held in place by more than nicotine: the coffee, the walk outside, the phone call, the moment after a meal. Each of those is a learned cue, and cues respond to rehearsal — which is what a hypnosis session provides.

Sessions work on two fronts. They rehearse the cue arriving and passing without the cigarette, and they keep your own reason for quitting vivid, so the decision does not have to be remade under pressure every time.

What a session works on

  • Your specific triggers, if you name them — the morning coffee, the drive, stress at work.
  • Riding out a craving as a wave that peaks and passes, instead of a demand to be met.
  • Your reason for quitting, in your own terms — health, money, children, breath, control.
  • The identity shift from "trying to quit" to someone who does not smoke.

How Onira builds your session

  1. You pick a theme and, if you want, type a sentence or two about what is actually going on.
  2. An AI model running on your phone writes the script — induction, deepening, imagery, suggestions, anchoring, then a clear return to full alertness.
  3. Narration starts as soon as the opening is ready and the rest keeps writing while you listen, so a full 15 to 30 minute session begins in seconds.
  4. Keep a session you liked and replay the exact same narration whenever you want.

What to expect

Sessions run 15 to 30 minutes and end fully alert. Many people listen daily through the first weeks — the period when cues fire hardest — and keep a saved session for difficult days afterwards.

Common questions

Will one session make me stop smoking?

No. Onira supports a decision you have already made; it is not a cure and makes no clinical claim. Nicotine replacement, prescribed medication and cessation services are worth discussing with a doctor or pharmacist.

When should I listen?

Ideally before the times of day your cravings are strongest, and again whenever a difficult day is coming.

Does it work for vaping too?

The same structure applies — type in what you actually want to stop and the session is written around that.

Private by design
