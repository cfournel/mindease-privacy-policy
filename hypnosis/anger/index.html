Self-hypnosis for anger and a short temper

A session for the seconds before you snap: the heat, the clenched jaw, the sentence you will regret. Written around what sets you off.

Why a calm rehearsal helps with anger

Anger rarely starts at the moment you shout. The body is already tense, the jaw tight and the breathing short, and by the time you notice, the reaction is under way. Trying to argue yourself out of it at that point is the hardest possible moment to do it.

A guided session works earlier. It lowers the baseline tension first, because a calm body gives the habit less to feed on, then walks you through your usual trigger with a slower response. Rehearsed in a relaxed state, that response starts to arrive sooner than the automatic one.

What a session works on

  • Lowering the background tension that makes small things feel large.
  • The first signs in the body, heat, jaw, shoulders, which are the cue you can still act on.
  • A specific trigger if you type it in: traffic, a colleague, a child, a message.
  • The pause itself, rehearsed until it comes without effort.

Common questions

Can self-hypnosis cure anger problems?

No. It is a relaxation tool that can make the build-up easier to catch. If anger leads you to hurt someone or yourself, or feels out of your control, talk to a professional: Onira is not a treatment.

Is it for my partner or my child?

A session is for the person listening and works only if they choose it. Using it to change someone else is not what it is for.

How long before I notice a difference?

Most people feel calmer after the first session. A different reaction in the moment takes repetition, so plan on a few weeks of regular use.
