Onira vs Hypnothera: which one to choose?

Updated November 23, 2026

Both write a hypnosis session from your own words and read it aloud. Hypnothera bets on choice (voices, languages, lengths, web and iPhone); Onira on simplicity and privacy, with no account and no credits.

The short answer Choose Onira if you are on Android and want sessions with no account and no credits, written on the phone if it can run the model. Choose Hypnothera if you are on iPhone, want to pick from 100+ voices and 14 languages, or very long sessions, and don't mind credits.

Choose Onira if…

  1. You want no account, no email address and no credits to watch.
  2. You want what you type to stay on the phone, or to use the app in airplane mode.
  3. You listen in English, French or Spanish, on Android.

Choose Hypnothera if…

  1. You are on iPhone or want to listen in a browser.
  2. You want to pick from many voices, or a language other than Onira's three.
  3. You want very long sessions or to edit the script yourself.

Common questions

Is Hypnothera free?

Partly: a free account gets 25 credits, then 15 more each week (about one minute of audio per credit), plus unlimited quick sessions of 30 seconds to 5 minutes, according to its site on November 22, 2026.

Do I need an account for Onira?

No, neither for personalized sessions nor for ready-made ones.

Private by design

Everything can stay on your phone, if your phone can run the model: Onira runs it on the device, offline, and then the theme you pick, the words you type and the session you receive are never uploaded. If you prefer, Onira Cloud writes the session on Onira's own server instead, only after you agree, and the text is not stored. Either way there is no account.
